# Epic: Case Management

## Goal

Allow clients to view, search, filter, and close their own cases.

## Pages

- My Cases
- Case Details

## Stories

- CM-01: View My Cases
- CM-02: View Case Details
- CM-03: Close Case
- CM-04: Search Cases
- CM-05: Filter Cases
- CM-06: Paginate Cases

## Business Rules

- Clients can only access their own cases.
- Submitted cases can be closed.
- Closed cases cannot be reopened.

## Done When

- Clients can view and manage their cases.
- Search, filtering, and pagination work.
- All acceptance criteria pass.
